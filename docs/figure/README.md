# The header figure

A skein is a V of geese in flight. The lead goose is the point, and the four behind it are the members.

`skein.html` is the interactive version, drawn with [Hairline](https://hairline.lucasmarkes.com). Download it and open it in a browser. Point at a goose: it raises its wings, and the beat passes down the V. Press **play** to watch the tour. `skein.js` is the figure's source.

The images in `docs/images/` are its rest pose:

- `header-light.png` and `header-dark.png`: the README header.
- `social-preview.png` (light) and `social-preview-dark.png`: 1280 × 640 cards for the repository's social preview (Settings → General → Social preview).
